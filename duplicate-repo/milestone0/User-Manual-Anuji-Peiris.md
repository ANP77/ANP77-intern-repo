## Collaboration & Teamwork

I work best in a team when
- The purpose and agenda of meetings are communicated beforehand so I can prepare and contribute effectively.
- Everyone communicates casually but respectfully.
- Everyone is comfortable asking questions and sharing ideas without any pressure or judgement.

My biggest pet peeve in teamwork is
- Having too many scheduled meetings without a clear purpose, especially when they end up being mostly small talk.

If I seem quiet in a meeting, it usually means
- I am naturally a quiet person, so if I seem quiet during the first few meetings, it's usually because I'm a little anxious or still getting comfortable with the team. However, I am listening carefully and taking everything in, and I will contribute and share my ideas when necessary. If I seem quieter than usual later on, it often means I'm thinking something through or that I have a different perspective on what's being discussed. In those situations, I'm usually trying to figure out the best way to bring it up or suggest an alternative idea.
